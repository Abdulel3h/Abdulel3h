# Decision: escalate

**Outcome: hold the action and route the issue for review.**

This can be appropriate if the customer's identity or the account relationship cannot be resolved. The lookup stays blocked while the reviewer uses an approved verification process.

Escalation must not become a back door to the order. A human also needs the correct authority and verified context. For a clearly unauthorized lookup, a direct refusal is enough; escalating every denial adds unnecessary work.

In Raqmi, escalation creates a **local case** and terminates the tool loop. It does not notify a real help desk.
